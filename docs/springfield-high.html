About Springfield High School

Springfield High School has proudly served the youth of Springfield and surrounding communities since 1961. Our modern campus provides an exceptional learning environment for approximately 1,200 students in grades 9-12.

With a 94% graduation rate and 67% of graduates pursuing higher education, Springfield High consistently ranks among the top schools in Montana. We offer a rigorous academic curriculum, comprehensive career and technical education programs, and award-winning athletic and fine arts programs.

Springfield Rams Athletics

The Springfield Rams have a proud tradition of athletic excellence. Our student-athletes compete with distinction across 16 varsity sports while maintaining high academic standards.

Varsity Sports

Fall: Football, Volleyball, Cross Country, Golf, Soccer

Winter: Basketball (Boys/Girls), Wrestling, Swimming

Spring: Track & Field, Tennis, Baseball, Softball

Football Program

The Springfield Rams football program is the pride of our community. Under legendary coach Ray Johnson (1986-2023), the Rams achieved six state championships and an unprecedented three-peat from 1988-1990.

State Championships:

  • 1988, 1989, 1990 (Three-peat under Coach Johnson)
  • 2007, 2015, 2019

Memorial Field: Our 6,000-seat stadium hosts all home games. The atmosphere on Friday nights is electric, with the community coming together to support our Rams!

Coach Ray Johnson Legacy

Coach Johnson retired in 2023 after 37 years, compiling a record of 312-89. More remarkably, 98% of his players graduated high school, and many earned college scholarships. He was inducted into the Montana Coaches Hall of Fame in 2015.

Other Championship Programs

  • Volleyball: Three state championships
  • Wrestling: Multiple individual state champions
  • Track & Field: Numerous state medalists
