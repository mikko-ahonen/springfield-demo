City Council

City Council meetings are held the first and third Mondays of each month at 7:00 PM in City Hall Council Chambers. Meetings are open to the public.

Public Comment

Citizens may address the City Council during the public comment period at the beginning of each meeting. Each speaker is limited to three minutes. To be added to the agenda for a specific item, contact the City Clerk at (555) 555-1020 by noon on the Friday before the meeting.
