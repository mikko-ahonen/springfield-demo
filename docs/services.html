Utility Services

The City of Springfield provides water and sewer services to approximately 12,400 connections.

Pay Your Utility Bill

  • Online: Pay Online (24/7)
  • By Phone: (555) 555-2150 (automated system)
  • By Mail: City of Springfield, PO Box 1886, Springfield, MT 59401
  • In Person: City Hall, Finance Counter, Room 201
  • Drop Box: City Hall parking lot (24/7)

Utility Services

  • Start/Stop Water Service
  • Report a Water Leak
  • Water Quality Reports
  • Utility Rate Schedule

Water Emergency?

For water main breaks or sewer backups, call Public Works Emergency Line: (555) 555-6099 (available 24/7)

Parks & Recreation

Springfield Parks & Recreation manages 340 acres of parkland across 18 parks, plus trails and sports facilities.

Facilities

  • Petersen Community Pool - Indoor pool, lap swimming, lessons, water aerobics
  • Springfield Sports Complex - Baseball/softball fields, soccer, tennis
  • Riverside Park - Trails, picnic areas, disc golf, river access
  • Lake Springfield - Fishing, boating, swimming, camping
  • Recreation Center - Gym, fitness classes, meeting rooms
