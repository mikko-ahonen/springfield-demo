Human Resources Department

Supporting Springfield's workforce through recruitment, benefits, training, and employee services

Department Overview

The Human Resources Department serves as the central point for all employee-related services for the City of Springfield. We support approximately 450 full-time equivalent employees across 16 departments, managing the complete employee lifecycle from recruitment through retirement.

Our mission is to attract, develop, and retain a skilled and diverse workforce dedicated to providing excellent public service to the citizens of Springfield.

Employee Benefits

The City of Springfield offers a comprehensive benefits package to eligible employees:

Health & Wellness

  • Medical insurance (PPO and HDHP options)
  • Dental insurance
  • Vision insurance
  • Employee Assistance Program (EAP)
  • Wellness program with incentives
  • Life insurance (basic and supplemental)
  • Long-term disability insurance

Retirement

  • Montana Public Employees' Retirement System (PERS)
  • Optional 457(b) deferred compensation plan
  • Social Security participation

Time Off

  • Paid vacation leave (accrued based on years of service)
  • Paid sick leave
  • 11 paid holidays per year
  • Personal leave day
  • Bereavement leave
  • Jury duty leave
  • Military leave

Additional Benefits

  • Tuition reimbursement program
  • Professional development opportunities
  • Flexible spending accounts (FSA)
  • Employee recognition programs
  • Direct deposit

New Employee Onboarding

Welcome to the City of Springfield! Our comprehensive onboarding program ensures new employees have everything they need to succeed from day one.

Before Your First Day

HR will contact you to complete pre-employment paperwork, schedule your orientation, and coordinate with your department and IT for equipment setup.

Your First Day

  • Report to HR at 8:00 AM (City Hall, Room 203)
  • Complete required documentation (I-9, tax forms, benefits enrollment)
  • Receive employee ID badge
  • Meet with IT for computer and system access
  • Meet your supervisor and tour your department

First Week

  • New Employee Orientation (NEO) - typically held second Tuesday of month
  • Department-specific training
  • Review of policies and procedures
  • Safety training (as required)

Questions about onboarding? Contact HR Specialist Sarah Chen at (555) 555-4002.

Why Work for Springfield?

The City of Springfield has been recognized as one of Montana's best places to work in local government. Our employees enjoy:

  • Meaningful Work: Direct impact on your community and neighbors
  • Job Security: Stable employment with a respected public institution
  • Work-Life Balance: Reasonable hours and generous leave policies
  • Excellent Benefits: Comprehensive health, retirement, and wellness programs
  • Career Growth: Opportunities for advancement and professional development
  • Great Location: Beautiful Montana setting with low cost of living
