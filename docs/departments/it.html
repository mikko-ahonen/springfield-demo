Information Technology Department

Providing technology infrastructure, systems management, cybersecurity, and end-user support for Springfield Municipality

Department Overview

The IT Department provides comprehensive technology services to enable efficient municipal operations and public service delivery. We support approximately 500+ municipal employees across 16 departments with over 600 endpoints, 45 servers, and 200+ applications.

Our team maintains critical infrastructure including the municipal data center, wide-area network connecting 12 facilities, voice communications systems, public safety dispatch systems, and citizen-facing digital services.

Response Time Standards

We are committed to resolving your technology issues promptly. Our service level targets:

Priority Description Response Resolution
P1 - Critical Major system outage affecting multiple users 15 minutes 2 hours
P2 - High Significant impact on productivity 1 hour 4 hours
P3 - Medium Moderate impact, workaround available 4 hours 8 hours
P4 - Low Minimal impact, informational 1 day 3 days

Equipment Replacement

If your municipal laptop or desktop computer is broken, malfunctioning, or has reached end-of-life, the IT Department will work to get you back up and running quickly:

  • Loaner Device: Available within 4 hours of reporting your issue
  • Permanent Replacement: Deployed within 5 business days
  • Data Recovery: We will recover your files whenever possible

To request equipment replacement, contact the IT Help Desk by phone at (555) 555-5000 or email helpdesk@springfieldmunicipality.com.

Equipment Standards

Standard laptop lifecycle is 4 years; desktops are 5 years. Equipment meeting these thresholds is eligible for proactive replacement during annual refresh cycles.

Cybersecurity

Protecting municipal technology assets and citizen data is a top priority. The IT Security Officer manages our cybersecurity program including:

  • 24/7 security monitoring and threat detection
  • Annual security awareness training for all employees
  • Phishing simulation campaigns
  • Vulnerability management and patching
  • Access control and permission management
  • Compliance with CJIS, PCI, and other standards

Report a Security Concern

If you receive a suspicious email, notice unusual computer behavior, or have any security concerns, report immediately:

  • Email: security@springfieldmunicipality.com
  • Phone: (555) 555-5003

Do not click links or open attachments in suspicious emails.
