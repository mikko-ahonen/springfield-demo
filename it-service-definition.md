# Service Definition: Information Technology Service

## Service Overview

- **Service Name:** IT Services
- **Service Version:** 1.0
- **Internal Name:** it-services
- **Service Type:** Technology Infrastructure and Support
- **Status:** Active
- **Provider Organization:** Information Technology Department, Springfield Municipality
- **Service Owner:** IT Director

## Service Description

The IT Service provides comprehensive technology infrastructure, systems management, cybersecurity, and end-user support for Springfield Municipality. The service encompasses hardware provisioning, software management, network operations, data security, help desk support, and technology training to enable efficient municipal operations and public service delivery.

This service ensures that Springfield Municipality employees have reliable, secure, and modern technology tools to perform their duties effectively. The IT Service acts as both an operational backbone and strategic enabler, supporting approximately 500+ municipal employees across 16 departments with over 600 endpoints, 45 servers, and 200+ applications.

The IT Department maintains critical infrastructure including the municipal data center, wide-area network connecting 12 facilities, voice communications systems, public safety dispatch systems, and citizen-facing digital services. The service operates 24/7 for critical systems with standard help desk support during business hours.

## Service Routines

### Equipment Replacement Routine

- **Description:** Comprehensive process for replacing broken, malfunctioning, or end-of-life employee computing equipment (laptops, desktops) to minimize productivity disruption and ensure employees have reliable technology tools. The routine encompasses initial report, assessment, loaner issuance, data migration, permanent replacement, and asset disposal.

- **Trigger:** Employee reports equipment malfunction, damage, or failure via help desk ticket, phone, email, or in-person

- **Frequency:** Approximately 8-12 equipment replacements per month across the municipality (combination of repairs, replacements, and standard lifecycle refreshes)

- **Duration:** Loaner device within 4 hours; permanent replacement within 5 business days

- **Participants:**
  - **Employee:** Reports issue, uses loaner, verifies data restoration, returns loaner
  - **IT Help Desk Technician:** Receives report, assesses issue, issues loaner, processes permanent replacement
  - **IT Administrator:** Reviews assessments, approves replacements, manages inventory, handles complex cases
  - **IT Director:** Approves emergency purchases, reviews equipment budget, handles escalations
  - **Hiring Supervisor/Department Head:** Approves replacement if budget impact, confirms business need
  - **Finance Department:** Processes purchase orders, tracks asset depreciation
  - **Vendor (Dell, HP):** Provides warranty repairs, fulfills equipment orders

#### Process Steps Overview

The Equipment Replacement routine consists of 18 major process steps. Detailed step-by-step procedures are documented in the Springfield Municipality Broken Laptop Replacement Process (see reference: `/src/broken-laptop-process.md`).

**Phase 1: Initial Report and Assessment (Hours 0-4)**

1. **Issue Report and Ticket Creation** (Immediate)
   - Responsible: Employee, IT Help Desk Technician
   - Employee reports broken/malfunctioning equipment via phone, email, portal, or in-person
   - Help Desk creates ticket with detailed symptoms, captures equipment serial number and asset tag

2. **Initial Triage and Priority Assignment** (Within 30 minutes)
   - Responsible: IT Help Desk Technician
   - Assess urgency based on employee role and impact
   - Assign priority level (P1-P4)
   - Determine if remote troubleshooting possible

3. **Remote Troubleshooting Attempt** (Within 1 hour, if applicable)
   - Responsible: IT Help Desk Technician
   - Attempt remote diagnosis and resolution for software/configuration issues
   - Document troubleshooting steps attempted

4. **Physical Assessment and Diagnosis** (Within 2 hours)
   - Responsible: IT Help Desk Technician
   - Inspect equipment in-person or request employee bring to Help Desk
   - Diagnose root cause (hardware failure, damage, software corruption)
   - Determine repair vs. replace decision

**Phase 2: Loaner Device Issuance (Hours 2-4)**

5. **Loaner Device Selection and Preparation** (Within 30 minutes of assessment)
   - Responsible: IT Help Desk Technician
   - Select appropriate loaner from inventory based on job requirements
   - Verify loaner is functional, updated, and clean of previous user data
   - Record loaner asset tag and serial number

6. **Loaner Device Configuration** (Within 1 hour)
   - Responsible: IT Help Desk Technician
   - Configure loaner with employee's Active Directory account
   - Install department-specific software as needed
   - Configure email, printers, and network drives
   - Set up VPN if employee works remotely

7. **Data Assessment and Migration Plan** (During loaner setup)
   - Responsible: IT Help Desk Technician
   - Determine if data recoverable from broken device
   - Plan data migration approach (cloud sync, local backup, data recovery)
   - Communicate data status to employee

8. **Loaner Issuance and User Training** (Within 4 hours of report)
   - Responsible: IT Help Desk Technician
   - Issue loaner device to employee with checkout documentation
   - Provide brief orientation on loaner use
   - Explain data migration status and timeline
   - Set expectations for permanent replacement timeline

**Phase 3: Replacement Decision and Procurement (Days 1-3)**

9. **Repair vs. Replace Analysis** (Within 1 business day)
   - Responsible: IT Administrator
   - Review diagnosis and repair estimate
   - Check equipment age against lifecycle standard (4 years laptop, 5 years desktop)
   - Calculate repair cost vs. replacement cost
   - Make repair/replace recommendation

10. **Warranty and Vendor Coordination** (If repair path chosen)
    - Responsible: IT Help Desk Technician
    - Check warranty status and coverage
    - Contact vendor for warranty repair or parts
    - Coordinate repair timeline

11. **Replacement Approval and Procurement** (If replacement path chosen)
    - Responsible: IT Administrator, IT Director (if >$1,500)
    - Select replacement equipment per standard configurations
    - Obtain necessary approvals based on cost thresholds
    - Submit purchase requisition to Finance
    - Place order with vendor

**Phase 4: Permanent Device Setup (Days 3-5)**

12. **New Equipment Receipt and Inspection** (Upon delivery)
    - Responsible: IT Help Desk Technician
    - Receive and inspect new equipment
    - Verify specifications match order
    - Record asset tag and serial number in inventory system

13. **Device Configuration and Software Installation** (1-2 days)
    - Responsible: IT Help Desk Technician
    - Apply standard image with operating system and base software
    - Join to Active Directory domain
    - Install department-specific applications
    - Configure security settings, encryption, and endpoint protection
    - Install printers, configure email, set up network drives

14. **Data Migration to New Device** (During configuration)
    - Responsible: IT Help Desk Technician
    - Migrate data from broken device (if recoverable) or restore from backup
    - Sync cloud storage (OneDrive, SharePoint)
    - Verify critical files and settings transferred
    - Test data accessibility

**Phase 5: Deployment and Closure (Day 5)**

15. **User Notification and Deployment Scheduling** (Day 4-5)
    - Responsible: IT Help Desk Technician
    - Contact employee to schedule device swap
    - Coordinate convenient time to minimize work disruption
    - Prepare deployment documentation

16. **Device Swap and User Verification** (Day 5)
    - Responsible: IT Help Desk Technician
    - Meet with employee to swap loaner for permanent device
    - Employee verifies data, applications, and settings are correct
    - Provide brief orientation on any new features or differences
    - Collect loaner device

17. **Loaner Device Return Processing** (Within 2 days of swap)
    - Responsible: IT Help Desk Technician
    - Wipe user data from loaner device
    - Update loaner to latest patches
    - Return loaner to ready inventory
    - Update asset tracking system

18. **Old Equipment Disposal and Ticket Closure** (Within 1 week)
    - Responsible: IT Help Desk Technician, IT Administrator
    - Secure wipe of old device (DoD 5220.22-M standard)
    - Process for disposal or recycling through certified vendor
    - Update asset inventory (dispose old asset)
    - Close help desk ticket with resolution notes
    - Send customer satisfaction survey

- Provide detailed information about issues (symptoms, error messages, steps to reproduce)

- Follow IT security policies (password requirements, acceptable use)
- Complete required security awareness training annually
- Back up important files to network drives or OneDrive (not just local storage)
- Allow IT access to equipment for maintenance and support
- Return loaner equipment promptly when permanent replacement is ready
- Verify data and functionality after equipment replacement
- Provide feedback on IT service quality via surveys

**Related Routines:** Equipment Replacement (Steps 1, 8, 16)

**Success Criteria:** Minimal technology disruption to work, timely issue resolution, satisfaction with IT support

## Service Dependencies

### Technology Systems

**Critical Systems:**

1. **Active Directory / Microsoft Entra ID**
   - **Purpose:** User authentication, authorization, identity management
   - **Dependency:** All system access depends on AD; outage prevents logins
   - **Uptime Requirement:** 99.99%
   - **Risk Mitigation:** Redundant domain controllers, cloud backup via Entra ID

2. **ServiceNow (ITSM Platform)**
   - **Purpose:** Help desk ticketing, asset management, service catalog
   - **Dependency:** Help desk operations depend on ticketing system availability
   - **Risk Mitigation:** Cloud-based with vendor SLA, email fallback for ticket creation

3. **Asset Management Database**
   - **Purpose:** Track all IT assets including equipment assignments, serial numbers, warranty status
   - **Dependency:** Equipment replacement requires accurate asset data
   - **Risk Mitigation:** Daily backups, integrated with ServiceNow

4. **Network Infrastructure**
   - **Purpose:** Connectivity between all systems, devices, and users
   - **Dependency:** Network outage affects all IT services
   - **Risk Mitigation:** Redundant paths, 24/7 monitoring, quick failover

5. **Microsoft 365**
   - **Purpose:** Email, collaboration, cloud storage for users
   - **Dependency:** Email essential for communication including IT support
   - **Risk Mitigation:** Microsoft enterprise SLA, phone/in-person backup for critical IT support

### Other Services

**Internal Service Dependencies:**

1. **HR Department**
   - **Dependency:** New hire and termination notifications for account provisioning/deprovisioning
   - **Required:** Minimum 5 business days advance notice for new hires
   - **Impact:** Late notification delays employee productivity, security risk for untimely terminations

2. **Finance Department**
   - **Dependency:** Purchase order processing for equipment procurement
   - **Required:** PO approval within 2 business days for standard requests
   - **Impact:** Procurement delays affect equipment replacement timelines

3. **Facilities Department**
   - **Dependency:** Physical workspace, network cabling, power infrastructure
   - **Required:** Coordination for new installations and moves
   - **Impact:** Workspace issues delay equipment deployment
